db.createCollection("studentdata")
Aggregation:
1.$match
db.studentdata.aggregate([{$match:{city:'Surat'}}])
2.$group (count students city wise)
db.studentdata.aggregate([{$group:{_id:'$city',totalstudents:{$sum:1}}}])
db.studentdata.aggregate([{$group:{_id:'$city',avgmarks:{$avg:'$marks'}}}])
3.$sort
db.studentdata.aggregate([{$sort:{marks:1}}])
db.studentdata.aggregate([{$sort:{marks:-1}}])
4.$project
db.studentdata.aggregate([{$project:{name:1,_id:0}}])
db.studentdata.aggregate([{$project:{studentname:'$name',score:'$marks',_id:0}}])


complete pipeline example using Aggregation Framework
1. group students based on their city
2. claculate avg marks of students of students in each city
3. sort the result in desc order of avg marks
4. display only the city name and avg marks
db.studentdata.aggregate([{$group:{_id:'$city',Averagemarks:{$avg:'$marks'}}},{$sort:{Averagemarks:-1}},
{$project:{city:'$_id',Averagemarks:'$Averagemarks',_id:0}}])


customers collection
Find customers who spends more that 50k rupees
db.customers.aggregate([{$match:{totalSpent:{$gt:50000}}}]
count customers in each membership
db.customers.aggregate([{$group:{_id:'$membership',totalcustomers:{$sum:1}}}])
total spending by state
db.customers.aggregate([{$group:{_id:'$state',totalspent:{$sum:'$totalSpent'}}}])
sort customers by totalspending
db.customers.aggregate([{$sort:{totalSpent:1}}])
display gold members name,city,totalspent
db.customers.aggregate([{$match:{membership:'Gold'}},{$project:{customerName:1,city:1,totalSpent:1,_id:0}}])
